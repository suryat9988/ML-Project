# PulseLine — what and why

PulseLine is a **clinic operations** slice, not a diagnostic model.

Patients hate paper tokens and not knowing when to walk in. Staff hate a whiteboard that only the person at the counter can see. PulseLine gives both sides the same queue: a token, a position, an ETA, and a staff board that can call, start, complete, pause, or mark no-show.

The “AI” is a **front desk**, on purpose. It answers hours, parking, insurance, and “what to bring.” It will not invent a diagnosis. Emergencies are sent to 911.

Stack matches CaseDraft so the two portfolio pieces read as one platform story: React kiosk, FastAPI, SQLite, Compose.
